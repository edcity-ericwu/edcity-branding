## Photography for the studies

The study pages currently use **no photography** — the panels, grain, blueprint
overlays, and gradient meshes are all generated in CSS/SVG/Canvas, so they carry
no licensing burden and can be tuned freely.

Do **not** lift images from reference sites (amita-oshiete.jp, digitaleducation.hk,
Sana, etc.). Their photos and brand assets are copyrighted; using them even in an
internal study misrepresents and creates a licensing problem. When the studies
need real photography, source it from the client's own library or a licensed
stock provider, or drop in placeholder tiles until then.
