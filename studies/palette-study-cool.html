How to read it

A bright blue backbone on a near-white field. The organisation comes from space and restraint, not from dimming the colour. One warm signal breaks through where an opportunity opens.

The three roles

Bright blue is the backbone — structure, navigation, actions, links. Saturated on purpose; at roughly 5.9:1 on white it also works as text. Deep blue adds depth and framing.

The field is near-white ground, a faint lavender-blue tint for quiet surfaces, cool hairlines, and near-black-blue ink — most of every screen. Kept genuinely white, never warmed, so bright colour reads as energy rather than heritage.

Spark coral is the signal — a bright warm point where an opportunity surfaces. Small share, and never body text: about 2.7:1 on white fails AA, so it stays in marks, fills, and large display.

Where the warmth went

The “Human Craft” layer does not disappear — it moves from paint to texture: photography, rounded forms, humanist type, and a warm voice carry it. The palette itself stays cool.

Caveat

Bright-blue-on-white is the default education-SaaS look. The escape is the specific blues, a genuinely luminous coral, gradients built from the scale, and the fabric carried in type and image — not the colour chips alone.

Signal & Light

The field stays flat and white. Energy comes from the signal — coral — and from light gradients mixed from the palette scale, the way Sana builds its studio backgrounds. One gradient surface per view, tied to a real moment.

Bridge tones — gradients only

Periwinkle #6E8BF5 Peach #FF9E7A

These live only inside gradients — periwinkle bridges blue to coral, peach is coral’s luminous tail. Flat, they dilute the system. Never a flat fill or a text colour.

Use a gradient for

  • The moment an opportunity is surfaced
  • Onboarding and first run
  • Milestone and progress moments
  • Marketing and campaign heroes
  • Loading and transition states
  • Ambient background behind an empty screen

Never

  • Navigation, headers, sidebars, cards
  • Buttons or interactive controls
  • Behind body text
  • More than one gradient surface in a view
  • The daily screens — dashboards, lists, forms
  • Bright dawn at full strength behind content — dial the opacity right down

Motion: the dawn mesh drifts slowly or reveals on load — never pulses. Energy is in the behaviour as much as the colour, and it respects reduced-motion.
