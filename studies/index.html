Unified study One page, one section skeleton, one set of copy — a palette switch (A/B/C) that swaps only colour, corner-radius, body type and load-in motion. Built to compare the options with everything else held constant. Structure modelled on sanalabs.com/products/sana-learn. compare
Option A Slate, Earth & First Light warm · grounded · editorial
Palette Slate-blue structure, greyed earth fabric, a burnt-orange spark. Includes the Spark & Light gradient rules. colour Layout Quarterre editorial language — warm cream, huge grotesque headline, serif body, sage rail, rotating badge. Colour-cycling menu; firm rising load-in. quarterre.com
Option B Clear Field, Bright Signal cool · bright · precise
Palette Bright-blue backbone on near-white, cool neutrals, one coral signal, light gradients mixed from the scale. colour Layout Sana-style structure — grainy mesh hero, product sections, testimonial. Prefaced by the dawn opening. Colour-cycling menu; quick clean load-in. sanalabs.com
Option C Bright Field, Two Points vivid · playful · front-of-house
Palette Vivid electric palette adapted down — sky-blue backbone, pale cyan/peach/pink field, two signal points (pink = opportunity, green = done). colour Layout amita-oshiete.jp container language — asymmetric panels, folder tabs, blueprint overlays, grain. Colour-cycling menu; springy pop load-in. amita-oshiete.jp
